About Us

Bolide Infrastructure Solutions Limited (BIS) is a Zambian-owned and operated construction company delivering integrated infrastructure solutions across the country. We serve a diverse client base that includes government institutions, commercial and industrial organisations, as well as private and domestic clients. Our core capabilities span civil works, structural steel fabrication, perimeter security, industrial facilities, and general building works. We combine technical expertise with practical project delivery to ensure quality, compliance, and cost-effective outcomes. From large-scale infrastructure projects to domestic developments, we are committed to building durable solutions that support long-term growth.

Our operations are guided by strong safety culture, quality management systems, and efficient project execution.

Selected Project Experience

Structural Steel Fabrication

We provide end-to-end structural steel fabrication and erection services for industrial and commercial developments. Our scope includes detailing, fabrication, supply, and on-site erection of steel frameworks. Projects are executed in strict accordance with approved drawings and engineering specifications. We ensure quality control at every stage, from workshop fabrication to final installation. Our steel solutions deliver strength, durability, and long-term structural performance.

Perimeter Security & Fencing

We deliver robust perimeter security solutions tailored for industrial, commercial, and institutional sites. Our works include clear-view fencing, razor wire systems, gates, and controlled access installations. Each installation is designed to enhance site security without compromising visibility. We use durable, low-maintenance materials suited for harsh operational environments. The result is improved access control and long-term site protection.

Industrial & Warehouse Infrastructure

We construct functional industrial and warehouse facilities designed for operational efficiency. Our services cover both internal and external industrial works, including structural installations. Projects are executed with careful consideration of workflow, space utilisation, and safety. We work closely with clients to meet operational and logistical requirements. The outcome is secure, efficient, and scalable industrial infrastructure.

Civil & Road Infrastructure

We deliver comprehensive civil and road infrastructure works for public and private sector clients. Our scope includes earthworks, foundations, drainage systems, and concrete works. Each project is executed in line with engineering designs and statutory requirements. We focus on durability, proper site preparation, and long-term performance. Our civil works support sustainable and reliable infrastructure development.

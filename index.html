About Us

Bolide Infrastructure Solutions Limited (BIS) is a Zambian-owned and operated construction company delivering integrated civil, steel, and infrastructure solutions across industrial, commercial, and public-sector projects.

Our strength lies in combining local market knowledge with international standards, supported by robust project management, safety systems, and quality control processes to ensure reliable delivery.

Selected Project Experience

Structural Steel Fabrication

Fabrication, supply, and erection of steel frameworks for industrial and institutional developments, executed in compliance with approved drawings and SABS standards.

Perimeter Security & Fencing

Clear view fencing, razor wire, gates, and access control systems designed to enhance site security and operational control.

Industrial & Warehouse Infrastructure

Internal and external industrial works including structural installations and space optimisation for logistics and warehousing operations.

Civil & Road Infrastructure

Earthworks, foundations, drainage systems, and concrete works delivering durable infrastructure aligned with engineering specifications.

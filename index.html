About Bolide

Built on Experience.
Driven by Results.

Bolide Infrastructure Solutions Limited (BIS) is a Zambian-owned and operated construction company incorporated in November 2023, led by Hamachila Muzandu — a construction and logistics professional with over 12 years of industry experience spanning structural steel, project management, and infrastructure delivery.

In just over two years of operation, we have delivered projects for government institutions, multinational contractors, mining companies, commercial organisations and private clients — across Lusaka, the Copperbelt, Southern Province and beyond.

We combine technical capability with deep local knowledge and international-standard materials sourced and accredited through our suppliers in South Africa.

What We Do

Our Core Services

Structural Steel Fabrication & Erection

Design, fabrication, supply and on-site erection of steel frameworks for warehouses, industrial buildings and commercial developments.

Perimeter Security & Fencing

Clear-view fencing, diamond mesh, game fencing, razor wire, gates and access control for industrial, commercial, agricultural and government sites.

Industrial & Warehouse Infrastructure

End-to-end construction of functional industrial and warehouse facilities, including internal partitioning and structural installations.

Civil & General Building Works

Foundations, earthworks, concrete works, drainage, roofing, paving, interior finishes and general building for residential, commercial and agricultural clients.

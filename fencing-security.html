What is the difference between clear-view and palisade fencing? +
Clear-view fencing (358 mesh) uses a welded wire panel with very small apertures — it is highly resistant to climbing and cutting, and provides good visibility for surveillance cameras. Palisade fencing uses vertical steel spear-topped bars and provides a strong physical deterrent but with wider gaps. For most industrial and government applications, clear-view is now the preferred specification.
How long does fencing installation take? +
A typical residential or commercial fencing project of 100–200m is completed within 7–15 working days, depending on ground conditions and access. Larger projects such as the Nakonde Border Post (306m) were completed in 10 days. We provide a programme at the time of quoting.
Do you work on government and tender projects? +
Yes. We are NCC Grade 5, Category C registered, which qualifies us for government tenders. We have completed work for the Ministry of Finance (via Trademark Africa at Nakonde OSBP), Simon Mwansa Kapwepwe International Airport and Zambia Sugar. We provide full prequalification documentation including PACRA, NCC, tax clearance and insurance certificates.
Do you do out-of-town fencing projects? +
Yes — we have completed projects in Muchinga Province (Nakonde), North-Western Province (Lumwana), Southern Province (Mazabuka) and the Copperbelt (Ndola). Out-of-town transport, logistics and accommodation are quoted separately and agreed before work starts.

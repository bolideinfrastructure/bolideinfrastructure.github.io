Common Questions

Civil Works FAQs

Do you build residential houses? +
Yes. We undertake residential building projects — both new builds and extensions. We are also active in the diaspora market, managing residential construction projects on behalf of Zambians based abroad. If you are building from overseas, see our dedicated Diaspora Projects page for how we manage this.
What is the minimum project size you work on? +
We do not have a formal minimum project size. We have delivered small projects like a custom steel dog house (ZMW 21,950) through to a ZMW 1.99M farm complex. What matters is that the project is clearly defined and we can produce a proper BOQ for it. Contact us and we will advise honestly.
Do you subcontract or use your own team? +
Our core team handles project management, supervision and key trades directly. For specialist works (electrical, plumbing, piling), we use trusted and vetted subcontractors who work under our supervision and quality standards. You deal with one team and one contract throughout.
Can you handle out-of-Lusaka civil works projects? +
Yes. We have delivered projects in Mazabuka, Ndola, Nakonde and on the Copperbelt. For out-of-town projects, travel, accommodation and logistics are quoted transparently and agreed upfront — there are no surprise additions to the bill.
How do you manage quality on site? +
Our leadership team — including the Managing Director — is personally involved in every project. We conduct regular site inspections, photograph progress at each stage and report to clients weekly. We do not disappear once the contract is signed; we stay on top of the project until handover.

Common Questions

Balustrade FAQs

How much do glass balustrades cost in Zambia? +
Our published starting rate for glass balustrades is K4,000 per linear metre, supply, delivery and installation within Lusaka. The final price depends on the glass configuration, post count, staircase angle and site access. We offer a free site visit and provide a full quotation before any commitment is made.
What is the difference between 5-line and glass balustrades? +
5-line balustrades use five horizontal stainless steel tubes between vertical posts — they are the more cost-effective option from K2,400/m. Glass balustrades use toughened glass panels for a fully transparent, modern aesthetic from K4,000/m. Both use 316 marine-grade stainless steel for the posts and hardware. The right choice depends on your budget, the look you want and the location (indoor vs outdoor).
Is the glass safe? What grade is it? +
We use 8mm toughened safety glass on all installations. Toughened glass is significantly stronger than standard glass and, if it does break, it fractures into small, blunt pieces rather than sharp shards. It is the correct specification for balustrades and is suitable for balconies, staircases and exposed outdoor applications.
Do you install balustrades outside of Lusaka? +
Yes. Out-of-town installations are available and are quoted separately based on the location, volume of work and access requirements. Contact us with your project details and we will advise on logistics and pricing.
How long does installation take? +
A typical residential staircase and balcony installation takes 3 to 7 working days depending on the length of the run and complexity. We carry out a site visit first, then fabricate off-site and install in one continuous process. You will have a completion timeline at the time of quoting.

Common Questions

Structural Steel FAQs

How long does a steel warehouse take to build? +
This depends on the size and complexity of the structure. A standard medium-sized warehouse (400–800 sqm) typically takes 6–12 weeks from fabrication through to erection and roofing. We provide a detailed programme at the time of quoting.
Do you provide engineering drawings? +
We work from client-supplied drawings or can connect you with a structural engineer. We prepare full Bills of Quantities from approved drawings and manage the fabrication and erection process from that point.
Do you work outside Lusaka? +
Yes. We have delivered projects in the Copperbelt (Lumwana), Southern Province (Mazabuka), Muchinga Province (Nakonde) and across Lusaka. Out-of-town transport, logistics and accommodation are quoted separately and agreed upfront.
What is your payment structure? +
Payment terms are agreed per project. We typically structure payments around project milestones — mobilisation, material procurement, fabrication completion and erection completion — so you only pay when work is done. All terms are set out in the contract before work begins.
Are you NCC registered for structural steel work? +
Yes. Bolide Infrastructure Solutions Limited is registered with the National Council for Construction (NCC) at Grade 5, Category C. This registration qualifies us for government and institutional tenders across Zambia.
